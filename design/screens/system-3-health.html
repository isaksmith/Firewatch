Five things that can go wrong

What you see, and what keeps working.

The satellite is late or down

What you seeA banner with the age of the data, and a gap on the map. Never "no fire".
What still works911 is still your main alarm. Line faults, weather and reports still count.

The co-op feed is down

What you seeLine fault input greyed out
What still worksThe satellite and public reports still score.

The burn log misses an entry

What you seeThe alert says "no burn logged here"
What still worksDispatch dismisses it in one tap. Each dismissal is logged.

No cell signal in the field

What you seeShort texts, under 160 characters
What still worksOffline map, the radio pager, and LoRa mesh.

The score is wrong

What you seeA score with its reasons
What still worksA person decides. Every override is logged.
