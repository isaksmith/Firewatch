A feature qualifies if its type is `Possible Wildland Fire` and it has at least two separate scans. Null type rows are counted separately and never qualify. Registered-burn suppression cannot be replayed (no burn history), so these are upper bounds. Each qualifying feature is one FYI text to the duty officer at Investigate; NGFS alone never pages.
## Comparison with alert_load.md

`backend/ml/alert_load.md` puts the existing text load at 30.3 texts per spring (2023 to 2025 average, all four districts). The NGFS measurement here adds up to 149 qualifying features over March and April 2026 across the same four circles, but 127 of them are in the Flint Hills circle (Chase), 117 of those in April, which is the prescribed burn season. Without Chase the other three districts total 22 for the two months. Many Chase features are likely registered or unregistered pasture burns; registered-burn suppression is not replayed here, so the Chase number is an upper bound and may be much lower in real operation.

This is one spring (2026). It says nothing about variation between years, and NGFS is in beta, so its detection volume may change. Do not read these counts as a forecast. No row had a null `type_description`, so that exclusion removed nothing this spring.
